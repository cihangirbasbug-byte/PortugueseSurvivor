# Portuguese Survivor Vision

## Our Mission

Portuguese Survivor exists to help children who move to Portugal feel safe, confident and welcome from their very first school day.

---

## What We Believe

Learning a language is not about memorizing words.

It is about feeling confident in real life.

Every mission should help a child succeed in a real school situation.

---

## Who is Pico?

Pico is not a teacher.

Pico is not an examiner.

Pico is not a narrator.

Pico is the child's first friend in Portugal.

Pico encourages.

Pico celebrates.

Pico never judges.

---

## Product Principles

- One Mission = One Confidence Win
- Learn through stories, not isolated words
- Encourage before correcting
- Real school situations first
- European Portuguese only
- Every lesson should end with hope

---

## Success

Portuguese Survivor succeeds when a child says:

"I can do this."
