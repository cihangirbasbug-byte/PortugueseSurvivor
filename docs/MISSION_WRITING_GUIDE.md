# Mission Writing Guide

## Mission Standard

Every mission should be:

- focused on one clear learning goal
- short enough to finish in under 3 minutes
- story-driven and emotionally warm
- grounded in a real-life situation
- introduced by Pico
- supported by vocabulary, practice, quiz, celebration, and a real-life tip

## Required Mission Structure

1. Introduction
   - Pico welcomes the child and sets the emotional tone.
2. Story Scene
   - A simple, visual moment that places the child in a believable situation.
3. Vocabulary
   - One or two meaningful Portuguese words or phrases.
4. Practice
   - A gentle guided speaking or recognition moment.
5. Quiz
   - A low-pressure check for understanding.
6. Celebration
   - Positive reinforcement and emotional success.
7. Real-life Tip
   - A practical takeaway for everyday use.

## Writing Rules

- Keep the language friendly and child-safe.
- Use Turkish for the UI and guidance.
- Use Portuguese only in the learning content.
- Never punish mistakes; offer hints and encouragement.
- Keep the experience focused on confidence and real-life usefulness.
