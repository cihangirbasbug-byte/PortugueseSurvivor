# Vision

Create a warm, confidence-first Portuguese learning experience for children that feels like a real first-school-day adventure in Portugal.

# Mission

Help children build practical communication confidence through short, emotionally safe missions that connect language learning to everyday life.

# North Star

Every interaction should make a child feel more capable, brave, and ready for real-world situations.

# Product Principles

- Confidence before perfection
- Gentle guidance over punishment
- Real-life adaptation over rote memorization
- Emotional safety over pressure
- Simple, joyful, and clear experiences

# Non Negotiables

- The app language is the child’s native language.
- Portuguese appears only inside learning content.
- European Portuguese only.
- No punishment system.
- Missions must be story-driven and emotionally supportive.
- The experience must remain child-friendly and accessible.

# Target Audience

Children beginning their first language-learning journey, especially those experiencing school and social situations for the first time.

# Persona

A curious, shy, but brave child who wants to feel safe, understood, and capable.

# Product Philosophy

Learning should feel like a caring companion journey rather than a test. Every mission should help the child feel more confident in real situations.

# Courage System

Courage is the emotional reward for trying, practicing, and showing bravery. It should reinforce confidence rather than performance.

# Mission System

Missions are short, story-based learning experiences focused on one real-life goal. Each mission should be emotionally meaningful, practical, and easy to complete.

# UI Philosophy

The interface should feel calm, friendly, and clear. Visual design should support emotional safety and readability for children.

# Audio Philosophy

Audio should be warm, natural, and supportive. It should help the child hear European Portuguese in a realistic and gentle way.

# Future Vision

Expand into a growing library of missions that support school life, family life, travel, and everyday confidence in Portuguese.
