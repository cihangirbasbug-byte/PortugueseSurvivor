# Audio Guidelines

## Core Rules

- Use European Portuguese only.
- Prefer professional, clear, warm recordings.
- Keep the voice natural and child-friendly.
- Avoid exaggerated or robotic delivery.

## Pico Voice

Pico should sound friendly, encouraging, and emotionally safe. The voice should feel like a caring companion.

## Character Voices

Character voices should be distinct but gentle. They should support the story world without feeling harsh or distracting.

## Grammar Support

The product should support both boy and girl grammar patterns where needed, especially for future content variation.

## Obrigado / Obrigada Rules

Use the correct form based on gender and context. The content should teach this naturally and respectfully.

## Future Pronunciation Evaluation

Future versions may include pronunciation support, but the current standard should remain human-friendly and non-judgmental.
