# UI Guidelines

## Spacing

Use generous spacing to keep the app calm and readable for children. Avoid cluttered layouts.

## Typography

Prefer clear, rounded, friendly typography with strong readability. Headings should feel warm and simple.

## Colors

Use soft, approachable colors that support trust and emotional comfort. Keep contrast high enough for accessibility.

## Card Style

Use rounded cards with subtle shadows and clear separation between content areas.

## Animations

Animations should be gentle, brief, and supportive. Avoid anything jarring or distracting.

## Accessibility

Use readable text sizes, sufficient contrast, and intuitive touch targets. Keep interactions simple.

## Responsive Layout

Layouts should adapt smoothly to phones, tablets, and larger screens without losing clarity.
